Functionally complete POS checkout — handles walk-in, member, group, partial, promotions, parking, and products. Visually a gray form with emerald selection states and no domain personality. Start with touch targets (functional blocker), then simplify the CTA label, then establish visual hierarchy between sections.

// PRIORITY ISSUES
P0

Touch targets below 44px minimum

Minus/Plus stepper buttons are 36px (h-9 w-9) and product quantity buttons are 32px (h-8 w-8). For a mobile-first staff POS used on phones during service, these are mis-tap prone. Lines 842-859 (stepper), 1110-1122 (product).

FIX: Increase steppers to h-11 w-11 (44px) minimum. Product buttons to h-10 w-10 (40px) minimum. Expand hit area with padding. Mode: /interaction
P1

CTA button label is a 7-level ternary chain

Lines 675-697 resolve through isPartialByPlayers, groupMode, assignedCount, needsPricing, selectedGroupId, effectiveCheckoutCount, and pricingGroups.length. Staff cannot predict the button text.

FIX: Extract getCtaLabel() with early returns. Reduce to 2-3 states: "Thu tiền", "Thu trước N người", "Đang thu tiền...". Mode: /interaction
P2

All sections look identical — no visual priority

Six SectionCards all use rounded-xl border border-zinc-200. Staff doing their 50th checkout cannot distinguish prerequisite (pricing) from optional (products, parking, promotions).

FIX: Give pricing visual weight as a non-collapsible highlighted block. Make optional sections visually lighter and collapsible by default. Mode: /relayout
P3

Invoice summary is 145 lines of conditional rendering

"Thông tin hóa đơn" section (lines 506-651) handles 9 different conditional branches inline with nested ternaries and IIFEs. Hard to maintain, hard to scan.

FIX: Extract sub-components: PlayTimeSection, PlayerBreakdown, PendingItems, ProductLines, ParkingDeduction. Mode: /refine
P4

Nested SectionCards create border-within-border noise

"Nâng cao" contains "Nhóm giá", "Số người thu", "Người chơi sẽ thu" — each in their own SectionCard. Double-border nesting with no structural gain. Lines 751-984.

FIX: Flatten inner sections with border-t dividers. Reserve SectionCard for top-level sections only. Mode: /relayout
P5

Button loading prop exists but isn't used

The Button component has a loading prop with built-in spinner. The checkout button shows "Đang thu tiền..." as text but doesn't pass loading={submitting}. Wastes the built-in affordance.

FIX: Pass loading={submitting} and keep only the static label. Mode: /interaction
P6

No domain personality in color

Zinc-gray + emerald selects + amber warnings + red errors. For a billiards hall, this could be any admin panel. The palette is the Tailwind default with no brand tint.

FIX: Tint neutrals toward a brand hue (deep green or warm wood). Even a subtle chroma shift in surface grays makes the UI feel authored. Mode: /recolor
